### 12. `cloudless-ses-to-espocrm`

**Purpose:**  
SES-to-EspoCRM Case bridge. This function processes inbound email stored by SES in S3 and creates EspoCRM Cases through the EspoCRM API.

**Architecture:**

1. SES inbound receives email for `cloudless.gr`.
2. SES writes raw MIME email to `s3://cloudless-ses-inbound/inbound/`.
3. S3 `ObjectCreated:Put` notification invokes `cloudless-ses-to-espocrm`.
4. Lambda reads the raw MIME object from S3.
5. Lambda parses the email with `mailparser`.
6. Lambda optionally links the sender to an EspoCRM Contact.
7. Lambda creates an EspoCRM Case.

**Runtime:** `nodejs22.x`

**Trigger:**

- Indirect SES trigger through S3
- Bucket: `cloudless-ses-inbound`
- Prefix: `inbound/`
- Event: `s3:ObjectCreated:Put`
- Lambda permission: S3 invoke permission exists for `cloudless-ses-inbound`

**Observed health before fix:**

- Last 24h invocations: `16`
- Last 24h errors: `12`
- Error: `TypeError: (parsed.text ?? parsed.html ?? "").slice is not a function`

**Root cause:**  
Some MIME emails produced non-string `parsed.text` or `parsed.html` values. The Lambda attempted to call `.slice()` directly on the value.

**Fix applied:**  
Added safe body conversion via `toText(value)` and changed body handling to safely convert `parsed.text`, `parsed.html`, or `parsed.textAsHtml` before slicing.

**Validation:**

- Code-only Lambda deploy succeeded.
- Deployed CodeSha256: `fKSICE3/21IWPjL2aZvX2fsd+nGgUKZezAFJpzv+bcw=`
- State: `Active`
- LastUpdateStatus: `Successful`
- Previously failing S3 object replayed successfully.
- Replay returned:
  - `StatusCode: 200`
  - `processed: 1`
- Logs showed successful EspoCRM Case creation.
- No new recent error datapoints after replay.

**Status:** Fixed, deployed, replay-tested, and committed.
